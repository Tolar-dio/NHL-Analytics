# Notes for Claude

This is Tolar's NHL analytics repository. The goal is league-wide analysis: team strength from xG and shot share, luck regression (shooting % and save % vs process), goaltender GSAx, deployment and line usage, special teams, and betting and fantasy hockey angles.

## Data

- Load the flat tables first: `data/tables/<season>/{games,events,shifts,players}.csv.gz` (pandas reads .gz directly).
- Raw API JSON lives in `data/raw/<season>/<game_id>/` if a field is missing from the tables.
- `scripts/fetch.py` downloads and rebuilds; `python scripts/fetch.py --tables-only` rebuilds tables from raw files without network access.
- The sandbox can usually reach api-web.nhle.com and api.nhle.com now (Oct 2026) with the repo's User-Agent, but the nightly GitHub Action is the source of truth for data; don't commit hand-fetched game data.
- `fetch.py` keeps re-downloading a recent game's shift charts until every skater's shift total matches his boxscore TOI (the feed fills in for hours after a game; six Oct 15, 2026 games were saved short). `analysis/validate_toi.py` checks season shift totals against the official stats-REST time-on-ice report in the nightly run.

## Conventions in events.csv

- `situation_code` is the NHL's 4-digit code: away goalie, away skaters, home skaters, home goalie. "1551" is 5v5; "1451" is a home power play 5v4; a 0 in a goalie slot means empty net.
- `x`, `y` are rink coordinates in feet from center ice. Which net a team shoots at flips by period; use `home_defending_side` to normalize before computing shot distance.
- For goals, player1 is the scorer and player2/player3 the assists; for blocked shots, player1 is the shooter, player2 the blocker, and `team` is the SHOOTING team (verified against boxscores); for faceoffs, player1 won.
- Shootout events have period 5 and period_type SO; exclude them from shot metrics.

## Working style

- Separate process from results. Flag small samples explicitly; single-game line stats are noise.
- Tag confidence: [Certain], [Likely], [Guessing].
- Any betting or wager tracking belongs in a separate private repository, not here (this one is public).

## Supplementary data

- MoneyPuck shots: `data/external/moneypuck/<season>/<game_id>.csv.gz` (their `xGoal` is the benchmark for our own xG model). Credit MoneyPuck.com in anything shown publicly.
- NHL Edge: `data/external/edge/<season>/skaters.csv.gz` (flattened) and `skaters_raw.json.gz` (full per-player JSON, includes per-area shot counts and record-game overlays).
- HTML time-on-ice reports (`toi_TH.htm.gz`, `toi_TV.htm.gz` in a game's raw folder) are the fallback when the shift JSON is empty; `fetch.py` parses them into shifts.csv. Overlapping JSON shifts are merged. Validated: every player-game TOI within 30s of the boxscore.

## Past seasons

- `data/raw/<season>/` and `data/tables/<season>/` hold each season separately; 20252026 is backfilled via `.github/workflows/backfill.yml`.
- `data/external/moneypuck_summary/<season>/` has MoneyPuck season summaries (skaters, goalies, teams, lines) by situation. Use last season as the prior for this season's shrinkage instead of league average; carry player priors with the player, not the team (rosters change).

## Analysis scripts

- `analysis/player_flags.py`: our own xG model (logistic, out-of-fold) plus skater and goalie tables. Run first.
- `analysis/onice.py`: 5v5 on-ice CF/xG/G and relative xGF% per skater.
- xG source: MoneyPuck's xGoal is primary (AUC 0.769 vs ours 0.752 on the same 2025-27 shots; aggregates correlate 0.95+). Ours fills gaps: `player_flags.py` trains on last season plus this one, out of fold grouped by game, with sequence features (time/distance/speed since the previous event, royal-road crossing, previous event type and team, shooter's shift time), which lifted it from 0.740. The same features did NOT help the live ESPN model (72 games: AUC 0.721 vs 0.722), so `xg_live.py` keeps its simpler set.
- On-ice attribution: a player is on ice for an event if start_sec < t <= end_sec.
- `analysis/props_data.py <season>` builds per-game tables into `analysis/output/<season>/`; run it for both seasons before modeling.
- `analysis/models.py`: anytime goal, 2+ points, and game totals, with last-season priors that follow the player. Modes: `season-backtest 20252026`, `prior-backtest`, `tonight` (reads `analysis/slate.csv`).
- Backtest results (2025-26, 1,241 games, 44k player-games, walk-forward): goal and 2+ point probabilities are well calibrated and beat a naive baseline every month (log loss goal 0.3920 vs 0.4008 since the role-based finishing prior, 2+ 0.2653 vs 0.2792; 3+ predicted 877 vs 880 actual; 1+ calibrated by P1_CAL). Game totals do NOT beat league average in a useful way (corr 0.08, RMSE 2.284 vs 2.290): treat totals as context and compare them with the market line, never as a betting edge. `analysis/backtest_static.py` rebuilds the Track record page's `site/static/backtest_20252026.json` from a season backtest.
- Model settings (Oct 2026 walk-forward tests, `K` in `models.py`): goalie factors shrunk much harder (Kg 300, Kg0 600; goalie seasons barely repeat), on-ice finishing `Kof` 50 (relative to league, last season carried at `Kof_prior` 150), which fixed the underrating of stars (2+ points for 0.9+ point-a-game players: 25.7% predicted vs 27.9% actual before, 28.2% after), `PTS_CAL` 1.015, back-to-back factor `B2B` 0.025 (tired teams got 47.4% of xG in 2025-26). Tested and left off: finishing shrinkage in shot units (no change), negative-binomial points (over-predicts 3+), smaller Kx/Ko/Ki. `P1_CAL` (Oct 18, 2026): a Platt correction on the 1+ points logit (a 0.0033, b 1.0924), fit on the 2025-26 backtest, held on 2026-27 with priors (log loss 0.58915 to 0.58796); depth players' 1+ points ran high. The site reapplies it when a goalie change reprices a player (`p1cal` in tonight.json). Also tested Oct 18 and left off (all within noise on 2025-26 goal/points log loss): NST three-season finishing (G minus ixG per attempt, +0.00019 overall, -0.00047 from January), three-season goalie GSAx (0.00001), last season's offensive-zone start share (+0.00006/+0.00007), and an opponent power-play-opportunity term (negative; already in the opponent factor). In early 2026-27 (3k player-games, with priors) Kof scored slightly worse on log loss but still fixed star calibration; the live record decides. Role-based finishing prior `Rf` 0.02 (Oct 20, 2026): forwards who play more finish better (2025-26 G/xG 0.72 under 11 minutes a night, 1.14 over 19), and the flat league prior had fourth-liners' goals 15% high and first-liners' 5% low; the prior now slopes 2% per minute a game around 16.5, renormalised (goal log loss 0.39235 to 0.39204 on 2025-26, 0.38082 to 0.38066 on 2026-27; fourth line 0.85 to 0.93 of predicted). Steeper slopes, a D slope and a role prior on on-ice finishing (points) were mixed or worse.
- Lineup context (`analysis/context.py <season>`, nightly for this season): per skater-game 5v5/PP/PK minutes from the shift charts, PP and non-PP individual and on-ice xG, 5v5 rank on his team, the two forwards (or the D partner) he shared the most 5v5 time with, PP rank; plus `tier_games.csv` (5v5 minutes and xG each team allowed against top-six vs depth forward groups). Tested walk-forward on Oct 20, 2026 (scratch harness, 2025-26 without priors and 2026-27 with) and left OFF the model because nothing held in both seasons: slot-based minutes when regulars are out (team's recent minutes by rank; helped 2025-26 mostly through a depth-forward level shift, hurt 2026-27), a promotion bump for players moving up (worse), conservation of team minutes (flat), splitting rates into power play and other play (goals worse), last game's linemates' quality (points worse in both), and an opponent's defense against depth lines vs top-six lines (worse in both, worst for depth forwards). proj_toi already weights the last three games 0.6, so promotions show up after one game. Use the context for writing and sleepers, not for prices, unless a new test says otherwise.

- Defense (`analysis/defense.py <season>`, nightly for this season; last season kept in `data/derived/defense_20252026.csv` because it takes 6 minutes): per skater, 5v5 TOI from shifts crossed with the events' 5v5 stretches, on-ice CA/xGA/GA and high-danger chances (xG >= 0.15) against, the same for his team while he sits (relative, negative is better), blocks, hits, takeaways, giveaways, penalties taken/drawn, PK minutes, and 5v5 faceoffs he was on for by zone (faceoff `zone` is relative to the event team, flipped for the other side). `cards()` gives this season next to last season, percentiles among his position (40 5v5 minutes this season, 300 last; lower-is-better rows flipped so a long bar is good; own-end share is context), notes from the bigger sample, and `site/data/defense_league.json` for the map. Front end `renderDefense()` in `site/depth.js`, between Value and impact and How he plays, with EH defensive RAPM and A3Z entry denial cross-referenced. Giveaway and hit counts depend on each rink's scorer; never call a player careless from giveaways (stars handle the puck most).
- Goalie pages (`site/goalies.py`, `#goalie/<id>`, `renderGoalie()` in `site/depth.js`): GSAx, save rate, by shot danger (low < 0.06 xG, mid to 0.15, high) against the league, rebound share (saves followed by a same-team unblocked shot within 3 s), shots-against map, start log, last season, EDGE areas, EH GSAx/GAR by season (credited); percentiles need 150 unblocked shots. Linked from the League goalie chart, team goalie tables, preview goalie cards and the player search (which now finds goalies).

- Player bios (`fetch_bios()` in `fetch_external.py`): NHL player landing for everyone on a roster, refreshed every 45 days (`BIO_MAX` caps a night), saved to `data/external/bios/<season>/bios.jsonl` (one `[id, bio]` per line, sorted, so nightly changes are small diffs; it replaced `bios.json.gz` on Oct 24, 2026 and `narrative.load_bios` reads either): birth date and place, size, draft (with pick in round), NHL teams by season, `career_prev` (career regular-season totals minus this season, so it doesn't go stale), and since Oct 24 `career` (every league season on his NHL.com page: `s, lg, tm, gt` 2/3, gp/g/a/p/pim/pm, goalie w/l/gaa/sv/so), `awards`, `hhof`, `top100`. ESPN injury entries now carry `kind` (body part) for the storylines.
- Draft and Central Scouting (`fetch_draft()`): `data/external/draft/<year>.json` for 2005 onward, picks from records.nhl.com (`site/api/draft`, which carries player ids) plus Central Scouting final/midterm rank (api-web `draft/rankings/<year>/<1-4>`, matched by name and birth date, then last name and birth date). Past years are fetched once, the newest weekly. `careers.jsonl`: landings of first- and second-round picks not in this season's bios (`DRAFT_CAREER_MAX` a night), so comparisons include players who never stuck.
- Wikipedia (`fetch_wiki()`): one Wikidata SPARQL query maps NHL ids (property P3522) to English articles (`data/external/wiki/titles.json`, weekly); the batched action API (20 titles a request, `exintro` plain text) fills `summaries.jsonl` (lead section, description, revision), refreshed every 30 days. CC BY-SA 4.0: shown as written, credited to the contributors with the article and license linked; the writers may use facts from it.
- NHLe (`analysis/nhle.py`, run inside `dossier.Context`): league factors from consecutive seasons (15+ GP in league A, 20+ in league B the next year, harmonic-mean weights) across bios and draft careers; leagues with few direct NHL pairs chain through one league (USHL via NCAA, J20 via SHL). `ALIASES` folds NHL.com's league labels (Sweden/SHL, the NCAA conferences, ...). Oct 24, 2026: KHL 0.73, SHL 0.65, AHL 0.47, NCAA 0.40, OHL 0.29, WHL 0.29, QMJHL 0.27. Selection bias (only players good enough to move) inflates the chained junior leagues; the UI calls it rough. Written to `data/derived/nhle.json`.
- Dossiers (`site/dossier.py`, Oct 24, 2026): `dossier` in `players/<id>.json` and `goalies/<id>.json`: draft card (pick, team, club, CS rank, picks before and after), the last eight pre-NHL league seasons with an 82-game NHL pace, NHL seasons, international tournaments (named by year: WJC 2026 is season 2025-26), awards, the Wikipedia lead, and `comps` (same position, picks within 2/5/10 of his, drafts 2005 to two summers ago: how many reached 200 NHL games among those with four-plus seasons, median points a game in the first 20+ game season, the six closest picks). `notes` are deterministic sentences. Front end: "Path to the NHL" (`renderPath()` in `site/dossier.js`) on player and goalie pages.

Names shared with celebrities (`AMBIG`) need hockey words nearby. Front end (live from raw.githubusercontent.com): "The buzz" on player and goalie pages, "From the room" on team pages, "Most talked about" on Pulse > League.

- NHL EDGE extras (`fetch_external.py`): a rotation refreshes each player every 5 days, at most 260 a night, from skater-detail, skater-shot-location-detail, skater-shot-speed-detail, skater-skating-distance-detail and goalie-detail. Per-area percentiles from EDGE are unreliable at small samples, so they aren't shown. `EDGE_ONLY=<ids>` refreshes specific players when debugging.
